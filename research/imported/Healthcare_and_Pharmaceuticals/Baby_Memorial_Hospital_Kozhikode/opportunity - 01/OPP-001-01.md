# OPP-001-01 — Specific Hospital + Workflow Discovery

## Baby Memorial Hospital, Kozhikode — Acute Ischemic Stroke Code / Thrombolysis Coordination

## 1. Discovery Thesis

The next discovery targets a high-consequence clinical workflow in a mid-scale Indian tertiary hospital:

> **Coordinating an acute ischemic stroke patient from emergency arrival through imaging, specialist assessment, thrombolysis eligibility decision and treatment initiation.**

The candidate hospital is **Baby Memorial Hospital (BMH), Kozhikode, Kerala**.

The hospital is currently described by its own website as having **490 operational beds**, including **100 ICU beds and 23 emergency-room beds**, with advanced diagnostic infrastructure and critical/emergency services.

Source:

https://www.babymemorialhospitals.com/hospitals/bmh-kozhikode

A National Board of Examinations hospital profile lists Baby Memorial Hospital as a private hospital with **600 beds**.

Source:

https://accr.natboard.edu.in/online_user/hospital_profile.php

This puts the hospital squarely within the intended mid-scale tertiary-hospital discovery boundary, although the discrepancy between installed/total and operational beds must be preserved rather than silently reconciled.

---

# 2. Why This Workflow Was Selected

Acute ischemic stroke is an unusually strong ART discovery class because treatment eligibility can depend on a narrow time window and because the hospital workflow requires coordination across:

```text
EMERGENCY
   ↓
STROKE RECOGNITION
   ↓
NEUROLOGY
   ↓
IMAGING
   ↓
ELIGIBILITY ASSESSMENT
   ↓
THROMBOLYSIS
```

The Baby Memorial Hospital research team directly studied a mobile-app-based coordination strategy for this workflow.

The published study reports:

```text
76 patients thrombolysed using the app
        ↓
Mean door-to-needle = 41 min
        ↓
89% treated within 60 min
        ↓
57% treated within 45 min
```

Historical comparison:

```text
100 prior patients
        ↓
Mean door-to-needle = 57 min
        ↓
67% within 60 min
        ↓
47% within 45 min
```

The mean door-to-needle reduction was **16 minutes**, and the difference was statistically significant after adjustment for age, sex and NIHSS score.

Source:

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32992177/

Full article abstract:

https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1052305720307370

**Status: VERIFIED.**

# 19. Why This Could Still Be an ART Opportunity

A common mistake would be:

> "BMH already has an app, so the opportunity is dead."

That conclusion is premature.

The actual question is:

```text
WHAT DID THE FIRST AUTOMATION SOLVE?
             ↓
WHAT DID IT NOT SOLVE?
             ↓
WHAT EXCEPTIONS STILL REQUIRE HUMANS?
             ↓
CAN ART AUTOMATE THOSE?
```

This is a potentially valuable discovery pattern because mature automation can expose the **next layer of manual exception work**.
