# OPP-001-02 — Exception + Human Investigation Reconstruction

## Baby Memorial Hospital, Kozhikode — Acute Ischemic Stroke Code / Thrombolysis Coordination

## 1. Checkpoint Objective

OPP-001-01 established Baby Memorial Hospital (BMH), Kozhikode as the specific hospital and acute ischemic stroke thrombolysis coordination as the workflow.

The central discovery fact is unusual:

> **BMH already implemented a digital coordination intervention for this workflow.**

The published BMH study reports that the app supported:

- rapid patient-parameter entry;
- NIHSS;
- thrombolysis checklist;
- dose calculation;
- team synchronization;
- notification of on-call members and team leaders about patient movement;
- radiological-image sharing.

The study found mean door-to-needle time of 41 minutes among 76 patients treated using the app, versus 57 minutes among 100 historical controls.

Source:

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32992177/

Therefore this checkpoint does **not** ask:

> "What manual stroke workflow could ART automate?"

It asks:

> **"What exceptions remained around the stroke workflow, how were they investigated, and what residual coordination layer might exist after BMH's first digital intervention?"**

That distinction is critical.
