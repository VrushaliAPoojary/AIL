```text

+
```

Source:

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32992177/


# 28. Integrity Statement

> **OPP-001-04 does not pass current direct operational pain validation. Current BMH evidence confirms that advanced stroke services remain active and that a BMH-associated acute stroke software ecosystem is publicly visible. Because the historical problem has already been partially automated, it would be methodologically unsound to promote another ART product without proving a residual current gap. Therefore OPP-001 is HOLD, with current pain UNKNOWN and ART opportunity status unresolved.**
