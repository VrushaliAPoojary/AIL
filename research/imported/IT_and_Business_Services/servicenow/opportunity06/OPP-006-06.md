# 1. Research Question

OPP-006-05 left one narrow hypothesis:

> **Some complex field-service environments may contain context combinations that cannot be represented economically through a finite set of policies, rules, objectives, weights, constraints, and overrides, creating recurring human policy-selection burden.**

This checkpoint applies the final evidence test:

```text
REAL OPERATING CONTEXT
        ↓
MULTIPLE POLICY OPTIONS
        ↓
NO SIMPLE TRIGGER
        ↓
NO STABLE DECISION TABLE
        ↓
EXISTING CONFIGURATION INSUFFICIENT
        ↓
HUMAN CONTEXTUAL JUDGMENT
        ↓
RECURRING DECISION
        ↓
MATERIAL CONSEQUENCE
```

The opportunity survives only if this complete chain can be supported.

---

# 2. Executive Finding

The evidence confirms that:

- field-service scheduling is genuinely complex;
- multiple objectives and constraints are real;
- emergency and exceptional operating modes exist;
- organizations use different policies for different conditions;
- human dispatchers can make exceptions and policy choices.

However, the evidence does **not** establish the critical final chain:

```text
COMPLEX CONTEXT
        ↓
POLICY SELECTION CANNOT BE
RELIABLY ENCODED
        ↓
RECURRING MATERIAL HUMAN BURDEN
        ↓
EXISTING SERVICENOW CAPABILITY INADEQUATE
```

More importantly, current ServiceNow documentation shows that its field-service architecture already provides several mechanisms that directly attack the proposed residual problem:

- multiple policies;
- weighted objectives;
- hard constraints;
- dynamic scheduling;
- automatic intraday optimization;
- event-driven optimization;
- scheduling attributes;
- group/territory qualifiers;
- attribute overrides;
- dispatcher-selected policies;
- manual overrides;
- exception handling.

Sources:

https://www.servicenow.com/docs/r/field-service-management/field-service-scheduling/create-policies-schedule-optimization.html

https://www.servicenow.com/docs/r/field-service-management/field-service-scheduling/configure-on-demand-optimization.html

### Final conclusion

# OPP-006 IS KILLED.

The underlying field-service complexity is real.

The proposed **ServiceNow-specific residual opportunity is not established**.

# 4. Test 1 — Is Field-Service Scheduling Genuinely Complex?

## Result

# VERIFIED

Peer-reviewed research describes real-life multi-skill workforce scheduling and routing problems in field-service operations.

One study describes a real electricity-distribution problem with two competing objectives:

- completing higher-priority tasks earlier;
- minimizing operational costs.

Source:

https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cor.2020.104908

Another peer-reviewed study describes field-service operations involving:

- technician skills;
- capabilities;
- local knowledge;
- cost;
- task priorities;
- consumer satisfaction.

Source:

https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cor.2021.105472

### Classification

**VERIFIED EXTERNAL EVIDENCE**

### Conclusion

The underlying complexity is real.

But complexity alone does not establish a ServiceNow gap.

> "Multiple objectives require a specialized reasoning engine."

is **not established**.

# 22. Why We Kill OPP-006

The correct conclusion is not:

That is sufficient to kill the opportunity under the research contract.

The investigation deliberately stops here.

```text
POLICY
+
OBJECTIVES
+
CONSTRAINTS
+
DYNAMIC OPTIMIZATION
+

+
```

> **No sufficiently strong evidence demonstrated that ServiceNow customers experience a recurring, material policy-arbitration problem that these mechanisms cannot adequately address.**
