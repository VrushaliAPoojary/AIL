# 1. Why OPP-008 Exists

OPP-007 was killed because the supplier-disruption workflow became too well covered by current ServiceNow capabilities.

OPP-008 therefore starts from a genuinely different decision domain:

> **Hospital-wide patient-flow coordination under constrained and changing capacity.**

This is not supplier management, procurement, order management, incident management, or workforce scheduling.

The initial question is:

> **When hospital-wide patient demand, bed availability, staffing, discharge progress, and clinical priorities change simultaneously, how do operational teams decide where capacity should be rebalanced and which patient-flow decisions should take priority?**

The purpose of this checkpoint is not to claim a ServiceNow opportunity.

It is to determine whether the decision problem is real enough to investigate and whether current ServiceNow capabilities already cover it.

A 2026 peer-reviewed multiple-case study examined hospital-wide patient-flow decision making across five leading academic hospitals.

The study used:
