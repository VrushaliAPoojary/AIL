# 1. Research Question

OPP-008-01 established that hospital-wide patient-flow coordination is a real operational decision domain.

This checkpoint asks:

> **Can one documented real-world patient-flow decision be reconstructed precisely enough to identify the information, constraints, alternatives, trade-offs, human judgment, and outcome that an operational intelligence system would need to support?**

This checkpoint does not establish a ServiceNow gap.

# 3. Primary Real-World Decision

The strongest directly documented decision reconstruction comes from a 2026 multiple-case study of hospital-wide patient flow across five leading academic hospitals.

The study used site visits and 157 interviews involving 112 informants.

Source:

https://doi.org/10.1080/09537287.2026.2655748

# 4. Decision Event

The study provides a concrete operational example from Hospital U:

> A patient expected to discharge that day was waiting for a laboratory task. The command-centre manager could identify the patient and barrier and coordinate the laboratory to prioritize the patient.

Source:

https://doi.org/10.1080/09537287.2026.2655748

### Classification

**DIRECTLY REPORTED EXTERNAL EVIDENCE**

This is materially stronger than a generic patient-flow example because it identifies:

- a specific patient state;
- an expected outcome;
- a blocking dependency;
- another department controlling the dependency;
- a central coordination role;
- a prioritization action.

# 15. Multiobjective Decision Evidence

A hospital bed-management study used one year of patient-flow data and discrete-event simulation to evaluate emergency and elective patient flows.

https://doi.org/10.1111/itor.12360
