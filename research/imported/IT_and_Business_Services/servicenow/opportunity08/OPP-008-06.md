# 1. Research Question

The surviving OPP-008 hypothesis is:

> **There may be a residual decision-synthesis problem at the boundary between multiple specialized hospital decision-support tools, where a command centre must reconcile conflicting signals and determine a system-level intervention.**

This checkpoint asks:

> **Do real hospital command centres actually have to reconcile information from multiple systems and sources before making patient-flow decisions, and is that reconciliation materially difficult despite existing digital infrastructure?**

This is the critical test before considering any specialized solution.

---

# 3. Strongest Direct Evidence: Seven Disconnected Systems

A peer-reviewed 2019 study describing the design of a hospital Capacity Command Center reported that patient-flow and hospital-capacity data were initially distributed across **seven unique systems**, most of which did not interface with each other.

The data could technically be accessed, but doing so required significant effort and time.

The command centre therefore integrated feeds from the seven systems into real-time tiles designed to make the information actionable.

Source:

https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcjq.2018.11.006

### Classification

# DIRECT VERIFIED EXTERNAL EVIDENCE

This is the strongest evidence found so far for the original cross-system fragmentation hypothesis.

---

The command-centre design included integrated views such as:

- forecast occupancy;

A hospital command-centre implementation integrated data from seven initially poorly interfaced systems into actionable real-time displays.

Source:

https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcjq.2018.11.006

### Reusable lesson

Before proposing new intelligence, determine whether the existing bottleneck is actually **integration** rather than reasoning.

## PF-23 — Verification Layer

Even after command-centre technology is introduced:

```text
SYSTEM DATA
      ↓
CHECK
      ↓
TRIANGULATE
      ↓
VERIFY WITH FRONTLINE
      ↓
DECIDE
```

Source:

https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK608696/

### Reusable lesson

A digital "single source of truth" may still require human verification when operational data are incomplete, stale or inconsistent.

---

## PF-24 — Specialized Tool + Command Centre

A hospital may build a specialized optimization tool for a narrow decision, such as surgical scheduling against projected bed capacity, while the command centre coordinates broader operations.

Source:

https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/09537287.2026.2655748

### Reusable lesson

The target opportunity may lie **between specialized decision tools**, not inside one individual workflow.

---

## PF-25 — Centralization Does Not Eliminate Local Judgment

The 2026 study shows that central orchestration and local autonomy coexist.

Source:

https://doi.org/10.1080/09537287.2026.2655748

### Reusable lesson

Decision-support should augment central coordination without assuming that every operational decision should be centralized.

---

## PF-26 — Technology Does Not Guarantee Outcomes

The Bradford evaluation found no consistent significant improvement in patient-flow or data-quality outcomes following command-centre implementation.

Source:

https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10566538/

### Reusable lesson

Do not use "digital transformation" as a proxy for business value.

Measure the actual decision and outcome.

# 42. Research Integrity Note

This checkpoint produced the strongest evidence so far for a residual operational issue, but it also killed an important overstatement.

We now have direct evidence that:

```text
MULTIPLE SYSTEMS
        ↓
FRAGMENTATION
        ↓
INTEGRATION
        ↓
HUMAN VERIFICATION
```

is a real hospital operating pattern.

We also have direct evidence that hospitals already deploy predictive analytics and specialized decision-support tools.

Therefore the opportunity is **not**:

> "Hospitals do not have integrated data."

Nor is it:

> "Hospitals lack decision-support technology."

The only remaining question is much narrower:

> **When existing systems disagree, are incomplete, stale, or optimized for different objectives, does a command-centre team repeatedly perform a material cross-system reconciliation before making a consequential patient-flow decision?**

That question now requires a **real documented decision**.

If we cannot reconstruct such a decision with sufficient evidence, we should stop expanding OPP-008 and consider killing it.

No customer is declared.

No ServiceNow capability gap is declared.

No willingness to pay is inferred.

No AIL intake is justified.
