## 1. Evidence Rules for P02

- All entries must carry a unique ID formatted as `EVD-P02-xxx`.
- No evidence may be fabricated.
- Every citation must preserve exact definitions, canonical source URLs, and an explicit verification level (`E0`–`E4`).
- Unsupported metrics, generalized vendor percentages, and marketing claims must be removed or classified strictly as `E0` hypotheses.
- Standards-defined specifications must be rigorously distinguished from trading partner implementation-specific mappings.
- Synthetic benchmark results from test suites must **never** be entered into this empirical register.

## 3. Epistemic Traceability Index

- **`CLM-P02-001` (`E0`):** Retained as `E0` hypothesis. Mid-tier and spot suppliers communicate predominantly through unstructured email/PDF channels rather than EDI 855. Prior URLs were inaccessible (HTTP 404). Empirical prevalence remains an unverified operational hypothesis pending field survey or customer data audit. Status: `UNVERIFIED_E0`.
- **`CLM-P02-002` (`E1`):** Standards specification (Provisional). High-level 855 scope is verified by official standard body catalog ([`X12 Products & Standards`](https://x12.org/products/transaction-sets)). Specific line-item confirmation codes (`IA`, `BP`, `AC`, `DR`) and header acknowledgment types (`AD`, `AC`) are supported by third-party reference dictionary ([`Stedi X12 855 Guide`](https://www.stedi.com/edi/x12/transaction-set/855), [`Stedi Element 668`](https://www.stedi.com/edi/x12/element/668), and [`Stedi Element 587`](https://www.stedi.com/edi/x12/element/587)); official ASC X12 element-level tables are paywalled behind X12 Glass licensing. Operational delivery delay is NOT synonymous with code `AC` (which verbatim denotes *"Item Accepted and Shipped"*). Plain emails contain no EDI syntax codes; status mappings are analytical classifications. Status: `PROVISIONAL_E1`.
- **`CLM-P02-003` (`E1`):** Standards specification (Provisional). Delivery tracking requires tracking distinct date qualifiers in segment `DTM` (Element 374: `002` *"Delivery Requested"*, `067` *"Current Schedule Delivery"*, `017` *"Estimated Delivery"*, `068` *"Current Schedule Ship"*). Supported by third-party reference dictionary ([`Stedi Element 374 Date/Time Qualifier`](https://www.stedi.com/edi/x12/element/374)); official ASC X12 element tables are paywalled behind X12 Glass licensing. Status: `PROVISIONAL_E1`.
- **`CLM-P02-004` (`E1`):** Public ERP technical specification. In SAP S/4HANA, order acknowledgment (`AB`) is stored in `EKES` and does not automatically update delivery schedule lines in `EKET` for MRP planning unless customized as MRP-relevant (`KZDIS`); partial shipments require split schedule line generation. Canonical links: [`SAP Help Portal: Confirmations in Purchasing`](https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_S4HANA_ON-PREMISE/af9e44f7f707474da828235299d63614/4b90be5391d14fa9e10000000a174cb4.html) and [`SAP Support Knowledge Base`](https://help.sap.com/docs/SUPPORT_CONTENT/mm/3362140417.html). Status: `PROVISIONAL_E1` (Standard ERP Architecture; pending live DOM rendering audit due to client-side SPA).
- **`CLM-P02-005` (`E1`):** Public ERP technical specification. Inbound shipping notification (`LA`) represents transit commitment and serves as the baseline for Goods Receipt; schedule lines must preserve balance-due traceability in `EKES`/`EKET`. Canonical link: [`SAP Support Knowledge Base: Confirmations Customizing`](https://help.sap.com/docs/SUPPORT_CONTENT/mm/3362140417.html). Status: `PROVISIONAL_E1` (Standard ERP Architecture; pending live DOM rendering audit).
- **`CLM-P02-006` (`E0`):** Downgraded and unverified. Claims of specific universal time savings (e.g., 10 hours/week) or fixed cycle time reductions (40–60%) are removed from empirical assertions and tracked strictly as unvalidated operational hypotheses. APQC PCF defines Process 4.2.4.5 (*"Expedite orders and satisfy inquiries"*), but quantitative reports and PCF pages returned HTTP 403 (Forbidden). Canonical references: [`APQC Process Classification Framework`](https://www.apqc.org/process-classification-framework) and [`CAPS Research Benchmarking`](https://www.capsresearch.org/resources/benchmarking/). Status: `UNVERIFIED_E0`.
- **`CLM-P02-007` (`E1`):** Verified legal standard. Contractual delivery date enforcement is bound to the contracted Incoterms 2020 point of transfer of risk (`FCA` origin vs. `DAP` destination). Verbatim definitions verified on [`ICC Incoterms 2020 Official Rules`](https://iccwbo.org/business-solutions/incoterms-rules/incoterms-2020/). Status: `ACCEPTED_E1`.
