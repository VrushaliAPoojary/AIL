# RFP-001 — Enterprise Service Platform

**Synthetic test document. Not a real tender.**

## 1. Background
Northstar Operations is evaluating a service platform for its internal teams. The organization operates across several offices and expects to review demonstrations before selection. Background statements in this paragraph are informational, not supplier obligations.

## 2. Technical Requirements
**T-01.** The proposed platform must provide a documented REST API for reading service requests.

**T-02.** The proposed platform must support CSV export of service-request records.

**T-03.** The supplier must provide a sandbox environment for acceptance testing before production deployment.

**T-04.** The platform should support configurable notification templates.

## 3. Security Requirements
**S-01.** The platform must support single sign-on using SAML 2.0.

**S-02.** The supplier must enforce multi-factor authentication for administrative accounts.

**S-03.** The supplier must provide an audit log of administrative changes.

**S-04.** The platform must encrypt data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher.

## 4. Privacy and Data Handling
**P-01.** The supplier must identify the geographic regions where customer data will be stored.

**P-02.** The supplier must provide a documented procedure for deleting customer data at contract termination.

**P-03.** The supplier should support configurable retention periods for exported reports.

## 5. Service Levels
**L-01.** The supplier must commit to 99.9% monthly service availability, excluding agreed maintenance windows.

**L-02.** The supplier must provide an initial response to severity-one support incidents within one hour.

**L-03.** The supplier must provide a monthly service-level report.

## 6. Legal and Contractual Requirements
**C-01.** The supplier must accept a confidentiality obligation covering non-public customer information.

**C-02.** The supplier must disclose any subcontractors that process customer data.

**C-03.** The supplier must provide its standard data processing agreement for legal review.

## 7. Commercial Requirements
**M-01.** The supplier must quote an annual subscription price in INR, excluding applicable taxes.

**M-02.** The supplier must itemize one-time implementation charges separately from recurring fees.

**M-03.** The supplier should offer a price-validity period of at least 90 days.

## 8. Evaluation Notes
Demonstrations may be scheduled after an initial review. The evaluation team may request clarifications. These notes do not add further supplier requirements.
