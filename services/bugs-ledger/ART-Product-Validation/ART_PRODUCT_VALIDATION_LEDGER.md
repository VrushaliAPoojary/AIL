## Operational Definitions & Status Separation Protocol

The validation pipeline enforces strict separation between Azure DevOps workflow state, internal ART operational status, and QA verification criteria:

| Dimension | Description | Valid States | Authority |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Azure State** | Exact current workflow state assigned in Azure DevOps | `New`, `Resolved`, `Closed`, `—` | Azure DevOps |
| **ART Status** | Operational classification assigned by the ART Validation Engine | `OPEN`, `FIXING`, `RETEST`, `VERIFIED`, `CLOSED`, `BLOCKED` | ART Engine |
| **Retest** | Formal QA verification status required before closure | `Pending`, `Required`, `Not Required`, `Passed`, `Failed` | QA Lead |
| **Severity** | Impact classification of the defect on production systems | `CRITICAL`, `HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, `LOW` | ART Triage |
